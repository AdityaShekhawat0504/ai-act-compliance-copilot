# Brightloop HR Cloud privacy policy (excerpt)

Brightloop Software GmbH, Beispielweg 12, 80331 München, Germany, is the controller for applicant data processed on its own careers page and a processor for its customers.

Customer data is used to train the shortlist ranker only if the customer has opted in to model training in the admin settings. Data sent to the candidate assistant and the job ad writer is not used to train any model.

Questions about our use of AI can be sent to ai-governance@brightloop.example.
